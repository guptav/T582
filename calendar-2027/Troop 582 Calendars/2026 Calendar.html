** Currently we have two advanced backpacking outings for the long weekends in May (Memorial Day) and September (Labor Day).
These advanced backpacking trips have a participant cap of 15 or 20.
If there are other ASMs who would rather lead a car camping trip one of those weekends, then we can have two outings.
For example, if an ASM will AIC Spicer Canoe outing during Labor Day, then we can do both the backpacking and canoe outings that long weekend.
