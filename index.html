Join Troop 582!

Scouts BSA Troop 582 is a large, diverse, very active, scout-led troop based in Saratoga, CA. It is our goal to help each scout grow into an independent and strong young person.

We are an outdoor activities club for girls ages 11 through 17, with opportunities for recognition and advancement through learning and practicing the tenets of the Scout Oath and Law.

T582 scouts gain experience in practical life skills, outdoor adventure, environmental stewardship, community service, and youth leadership.

Recent activities

  • Backcountry canoe camping
  • Backpacking – Black Mountain, Yosemite, & Philmont
  • Archery Campout
  • Snow Camping
  • Northern Tier Canoeing
  • Camp Chawanakee and Hi-Sierra
  • Rock Climbing • Ice Skating
  • Seabase Sailing + Snorkeling
  • Angel Island camping

Key facts

  • 60+ active scouts
  • 20+ trained adult leaders
  • 2 summer camps & 2 high adventure trips annually
  • 25+ outings planned in 2026
  • 25+ Eagle Scouts since troop began 6 years ago
