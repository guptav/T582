Your Adventure Starts Here — Join Troop 582!

Imagine paddling through backcountry lakes, summiting peaks at Yosemite, sailing the Florida Keys, and building friendships that last a lifetime. That’s everyday life in Troop 582 — a large, scout-led troop in Saratoga, CA for girls ages 11–17.

When you join T582, you gain real outdoor skills, earn awards, lead your peers, and become part of a community that truly has your back — nobody walks alone.

What you’ll experience

  • Backcountry canoe camping
  • Backpacking – Black Mountain, Yosemite, & Philmont
  • Archery Campout
  • Snow Camping
  • Northern Tier Canoeing
  • Camp Chawanakee and Hi-Sierra
  • Rock Climbing • Ice Skating
  • Seabase Sailing + Snorkeling
  • Angel Island camping

Why T582 is the troop for you

  • 🏕️ 25+ outings planned in 2026 — always something to look forward to
  • 🏆 25+ Eagle Scouts in just 6 years — we help you reach the top
  • 👩‍👩‍👧 60+ active scouts & 20+ trained adult leaders in your corner
  • 🌄 2 summer camps & 2 high adventure trips every year
  • 👕 Free gear when you join: T582 Class B t-shirt, troop patches & neckerchief
