Why Every Girl Should Join Troop 582!

Troop 582 is your ticket to an extraordinary life outside the ordinary — epic adventures, lifelong friendships, real leadership, and skills that stay with you forever. We’re a scout-led troop for girls ages 11–17 in Saratoga, CA, and we want you on this journey.

Join T582 and you’ll grow into a confident, capable leader — through outdoor adventure, community service, environmental stewardship, and genuine youth leadership. Nobody walks alone.

Adventures you’ll actually do

  • 🛶 Paddle backcountry lakes on canoe camping trips
  • 🏔️ Backpack Black Mountain, Yosemite & Philmont
  • 🏹 Test your aim at Archery Campout
  • ❄️ Conquer the cold at Snow Camp
  • 🚣 Canoe Minnesota’s Northern Tier wilderness
  • ⛺ Spend summers at Chawanakee & Hi-Sierra
  • 🧗 Rock Climb • Ice Skate
  • ⛵ Sail & Snorkel at SeaBase Florida Keys
  • 🏕️ Camp on beautiful Angel Island

Here’s what you get when you join

  • 🏆 A proven path to Eagle Scout — 25+ Eagles in just 6 years
  • 🌄 25+ outings in 2026 — something epic every month
  • 🗺️ 2 summer camps & 2 high adventure trips every year
  • 👩‍👩‍👧 A supportive community of 60+ scouts & 20+ trained leaders
  • 🎓 Life skills: leadership, navigation, first aid, environmental stewardship & more
  • 👕 Free welcome kit: T582 Class B t-shirt, troop patches & neckerchief
